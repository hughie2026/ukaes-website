Research Interests

A Broad Perspective on Scientific Research.

UKAES explores a range of research interests across epidemiological, biomedical, environmental and interdisciplinary natural sciences.

Our research interests are intended to encourage scientific exploration, knowledge exchange and collaboration across academic disciplines.

Research Scope

Our Research Areas

Six broad areas of scientific exploration and academic interest.

01

Epidemiology

Population health, disease patterns, risk factors and epidemiological research methods.

02

Public Health Sciences

Health determinants, disease prevention strategies and population-level health research.

03

Biomedical Sciences

Scientific approaches to biological processes, biomedical investigation and human health.

04

Environmental Health Sciences

Environmental exposures, health risks and interactions between people and their environments.

05

Biostatistics and Data Science

Statistical methods, quantitative analysis and data-driven scientific research.

06

Interdisciplinary Natural Sciences

Cross-disciplinary scientific inquiry and emerging areas of natural science.
