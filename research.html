Our Research Areas

UKAES embraces a broad and evolving scope of scientific exploration, with a focus on epidemiology, health sciences and interdisciplinary research.

01

Epidemiology

Population health, disease patterns, risk factors and epidemiological methods.

02

Public Health Sciences

Health determinants, prevention strategies and population-level health research.

03

Biomedical Sciences

Scientific approaches to biological processes and human health.

04

Environmental Health Sciences

Environmental exposures, health risks and interactions between people and their environments.

05

Biostatistics and Data Science

Statistical methods, quantitative analysis and data-driven scientific research.

06

Interdisciplinary Natural Sciences

Cross-disciplinary scientific research and emerging areas of natural science.
