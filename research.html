Research Overview

Advancing Epidemiological Knowledge.

Scientific research is a central academic priority of the UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences.

UKAES seeks to advance the understanding of disease occurrence, distribution, determinants and population health through epidemiological investigation and interdisciplinary scientific methods.

The Academy's research direction encompasses infectious disease epidemiology, clinical and translational epidemiology, environmental and population health, and computational and statistical epidemiology.

Explore Research Areas →
Our Research Philosophy

Evidence. Discovery. Scientific Progress.

We aim to promote rigorous research design, responsible scientific practice and the generation of reliable epidemiological evidence.

Our research direction supports scientific investigation that can inform understanding of human and population health.

Scientific Disciplines

Our Research Areas

Four interconnected areas provide the academic foundation for the Academy's research development.

RESEARCH AREA 01

Infectious Disease Epidemiology

Investigating the distribution, transmission, determinants and population-level impact of infectious diseases.

This research area addresses epidemiological approaches to understanding infectious disease dynamics, surveillance and disease prevention.

Research Topics
  • Infectious disease surveillance
  • Transmission dynamics
  • Outbreak investigation
  • Infectious disease modelling
  • Prevention and population health
RESEARCH AREA 02

Clinical & Translational Epidemiology

Applying epidemiological methods to clinical research, health outcomes and the evaluation of healthcare interventions.

This area connects epidemiological evidence with clinical questions and translational approaches to health research.

Research Topics
  • Clinical research methodology
  • Observational clinical studies
  • Health outcomes research
  • Risk factor assessment
  • Evidence synthesis
RESEARCH AREA 03

Environmental & Population Health

Examining environmental, social and population-level determinants of health and disease.

This area focuses on understanding health patterns across populations and the influence of environmental and social conditions.

Research Topics
  • Environmental epidemiology
  • Population health determinants
  • Health inequalities
  • Chronic disease epidemiology
  • Population-based research
RESEARCH AREA 04

Computational & Statistical Epidemiology

Developing and applying quantitative methods, statistical models and computational approaches to epidemiological research.

This area supports the analysis and interpretation of complex health data through rigorous scientific and mathematical methods.

Research Topics
  • Biostatistics
  • Epidemiological modelling
  • Computational epidemiology
  • Health data analysis
  • Predictive and statistical methods
Scientific Methods

Research Methodology

Rigorous research methods are essential to the development of reliable epidemiological evidence.

METHOD 01

Epidemiological Study Design

Study design, observational research, cohort studies, case-control studies and epidemiological investigation.

METHOD 02

Biostatistics & Data Analysis

Statistical inference, regression modelling, epidemiological data analysis and quantitative research methods.

METHOD 03

Evidence Synthesis

Systematic reviews, meta-analysis, critical appraisal and evidence evaluation.

METHOD 04

Computational Modelling

Mathematical modelling, simulation methods, predictive analysis and computational research.

METHOD 05

Population-Based Research

Population-level investigation, health determinants, disease surveillance and population health assessment.

METHOD 06

Interdisciplinary Research

Integrating epidemiology with clinical sciences, public health, statistics and related scientific disciplines.

Research Standards

Research Integrity & Quality

The Academy's research direction is guided by principles of scientific integrity, methodological rigour and responsible scholarship.

Scientific Integrity

Promoting honesty, transparency and responsible conduct in scientific research.

Methodological Rigour

Encouraging appropriate study design, valid analysis and critical interpretation of scientific evidence.

Research Ethics

Supporting ethical research practices, participant protection, data confidentiality and applicable research governance.

Transparency

Encouraging transparent reporting of research methods, findings and limitations.

Reproducibility

Supporting reproducible methods and the reliable evaluation of scientific findings.

Academic Independence

Promoting independent scientific judgement and evidence-based academic inquiry.
