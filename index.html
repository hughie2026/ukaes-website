Welcome to UKAES

Advancing Knowledge. Strengthening Science.

The UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences (UKAES) is an independent academic and research institution dedicated to advancing epidemiological sciences and related scientific disciplines.

Through scientific research, research-led education, professional development and academic collaboration, the Academy seeks to support rigorous scholarship and the responsible advancement of scientific knowledge.
