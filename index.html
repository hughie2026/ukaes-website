Welcome to UKAES

Advancing Knowledge Through Research and Education

The UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences (UKAES) is an independent academic and research institution dedicated to the advancement of epidemiological sciences and related scientific disciplines.

Through scientific research, academic education, professional development and international collaboration, UKAES seeks to advance scientific knowledge and contribute to the improvement of population health.
