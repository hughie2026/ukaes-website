Academic Programmes

Advanced Diploma Programmes

UKAES presents six proposed Advanced Diploma programmes designed with reference to postgraduate-level learning outcomes. Each programme combines advanced academic study with applied learning and an independent research project.

UKAES-AD01 Advanced Diploma

Advanced Diploma in Epidemiological Sciences

Explores advanced epidemiological theory, causal inference, complex study design and disease surveillance. Students critically appraise research, apply quantitative methods and develop an independent epidemiological investigation.

Indicative Modules

  1. Advanced Epidemiological Theory and Causal Inference
  2. Advanced Epidemiological Study Design
  3. Statistical Modelling in Epidemiology
  4. Infectious and Chronic Disease Epidemiology
  5. Systematic Review and Evidence Synthesis
  6. Independent Epidemiological Research Project
UKAES-AD02 Advanced Diploma

Advanced Diploma in Biostatistics and Health Data Science

Develops advanced statistical inference and computational analysis for biomedical and population-health data. Students learn model validation, longitudinal and survival analysis, responsible machine learning and reproducible research.

Indicative Modules

  1. Advanced Statistical Inference
  2. Regression and Multilevel Modelling
  3. Survival and Longitudinal Data Analysis
  4. Machine Learning for Health Research
  5. Health Data Governance and Reproducible Computing
  6. Applied Health Data Science Project
UKAES-AD03 Advanced Diploma

Advanced Diploma in Lifestyle Medicine and Preventive Health

Critically examines evidence on nutrition, physical activity, sleep, stress and behavioural determinants of health. Students design and evaluate preventive interventions and behaviour-change strategies for clinical and community settings.

Indicative Modules

  1. Advanced Principles of Lifestyle Medicine
  2. Nutrition, Physical Activity and Metabolic Health
  3. Sleep, Stress and Behavioural Health
  4. Behaviour Change and Preventive Interventions
  5. Lifestyle Medicine Evidence and Population Prevention
  6. Lifestyle Intervention Research Project
UKAES-AD04 Advanced Diploma

Advanced Diploma in Health Policy and Health Systems

Examines health policy formulation, comparative health systems, governance, health economics and inequalities. Students develop evidence-based policy analyses and evaluate implementation challenges, equity and resource allocation.

Indicative Modules

  1. Health Policy Theory and Analysis
  2. Comparative Health Systems and Governance
  3. Health Economics and Resource Allocation
  4. Health Equity, Ethics and Public Policy
  5. Policy Evaluation and Implementation Science
  6. Independent Health Policy Analysis Project
UKAES-AD05 Advanced Diploma

Advanced Diploma in Hospitality, Health and Wellbeing Management

Connects advanced hospitality management with wellness tourism, public health, guest wellbeing and sustainable service design. Students evaluate hygiene, risk, operations and the performance of health-oriented hospitality services.

Indicative Modules

  1. Advanced Hospitality and Service Management
  2. Wellness Tourism and Health Hospitality
  3. Public Health, Hygiene and Risk Management
  4. Guest Experience, Wellbeing and Service Design
  5. Sustainable Hospitality and Strategic Operations
  6. Hospitality and Wellbeing Management Project
UKAES-AD06 Advanced Diploma

Advanced Diploma in Bioinformatics and Artificial Intelligence in Health Sciences

Integrates computational biology, genomics, multi-omics and artificial intelligence in biomedical and population-health research. Students build reproducible analytical workflows, evaluate machine-learning models and address ethics, bias and data governance.

Indicative Modules

  1. Advanced Bioinformatics and Computational Biology
  2. Genomics, Transcriptomics and Multi-Omics Data Analysis
  3. Machine Learning and Deep Learning for Biomedical Research
  4. Artificial Intelligence in Epidemiology and Public Health
  5. Responsible AI, Data Governance and Reproducible Research
  6. Independent Bioinformatics and AI Research Project
Programme Framework

A Structured Learning Experience.

The indicative programme format across the six proposed Advanced Diploma programmes is designed as follows.

12 Months Indicative part-time duration
1,200 Approximate total learning hours
Online / Blended Indicative learning delivery
Research Independent applied research project

Programme Information

These are proposed UKAES institutional Advanced Diploma awards. They are not UK-recognised Postgraduate Diplomas (PGDip), Ofqual-regulated qualifications or formally validated UK Level 7 credits. Programme specifications and academic arrangements are subject to approval and development.
