Education Overview

Advancing Academic Learning and Research Skills.

Academic education and professional development are central components of the UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences' institutional mission.

UKAES aims to support advanced learning in epidemiology, public health, biostatistics and related scientific disciplines through research-oriented education and professional training.

The Academy's educational direction emphasises scientific reasoning, methodological rigour, critical appraisal and the practical application of research knowledge.

Explore Academic Programmes →
Our Educational Philosophy

Knowledge. Methodology. Academic Development.

We seek to encourage a learning environment in which scientific knowledge is developed through evidence-based teaching, independent inquiry and academic engagement.

Our educational priorities are designed to support researchers, healthcare professionals and individuals pursuing advanced scientific knowledge.

Academic Education

Areas of Education & Training

The Academy's educational framework encompasses four interconnected areas of scientific learning and professional development.

EDUCATION AREA 01

Epidemiology & Population Health

Advanced academic learning in epidemiological principles, disease distribution, population health and epidemiological investigation.

This educational area supports the understanding of epidemiological reasoning and its application to scientific and public health questions.

Learning Topics
  • Principles of epidemiology
  • Infectious disease epidemiology
  • Chronic disease epidemiology
  • Population health research
  • Epidemiological interpretation
EDUCATION AREA 02

Biostatistics & Research Data Analysis

Quantitative training in statistical methods, biostatistical reasoning and the analysis of epidemiological data.

This area focuses on statistical interpretation, research data analysis and the use of quantitative methods in scientific studies.

Learning Topics
  • Fundamentals of biostatistics
  • Statistical inference
  • Regression analysis
  • Research data management
  • Statistical interpretation
EDUCATION AREA 03

Scientific Research Methodology

Research-oriented education in scientific study design, research methodology, evidence evaluation and academic writing.

This area aims to strengthen the methodological capabilities required for rigorous scientific investigation.

Learning Topics
  • Research study design
  • Observational research methods
  • Systematic reviews
  • Meta-analysis
  • Scientific writing and reporting
EDUCATION AREA 04

Professional & Academic Development

Continuing education and professional development for researchers, healthcare professionals and individuals engaged in scientific work.

This area supports lifelong learning, methodological improvement and academic skills development.

Learning Topics
  • Continuing professional development
  • Research skills enhancement
  • Scientific communication
  • Academic writing
  • Research ethics and integrity
Learning Pathways

Flexible Academic Learning

The Academy's educational framework is designed to accommodate different levels of academic and professional development.

PATHWAY 01

Postgraduate-Level Learning

Advanced academic learning designed around research-oriented subject matter, scientific methodology and critical inquiry.

Postgraduate-level refers to the academic complexity of the learning content and does not itself indicate a degree-awarding programme.

PATHWAY 02

Short Courses & Workshops

Focused educational activities addressing selected topics in epidemiology, biostatistics, research methodology and scientific communication.

Courses and workshops may be developed to support specific learning objectives and methodological skills.

PATHWAY 03

Continuing Professional Development

Professional learning opportunities intended to support the continued development of scientific and research-related skills.

Specific CPD recognition or external accreditation, where applicable, will be identified separately.

Research-Led Learning

Connecting Research and Education.

The Academy seeks to connect scientific education with the methods, principles and findings of contemporary epidemiological research.

Research-led learning encourages critical thinking, evidence appraisal and the practical application of scientific knowledge to complex research questions.

Academic Standards

Teaching & Learning Principles

Our educational approach is guided by academic rigour, scientific integrity and meaningful learning outcomes.

Academic Rigour

Promoting structured learning, critical analysis and academically appropriate educational content.

Scientific Integrity

Encouraging responsible scientific practice, research ethics and transparent reporting.

Research Relevance

Connecting educational content with scientific questions and contemporary research methods.

Critical Thinking

Supporting independent reasoning, evidence evaluation and analytical decision-making.

Professional Development

Encouraging continuous learning and the development of professional scientific capabilities.

Academic Collaboration

Supporting constructive academic dialogue, knowledge exchange and interdisciplinary learning.

Important Information About Academic Qualifications

UKAES educational activities are distinct from the award of UK-recognised higher education degrees.

Unless a specific programme is expressly identified as being delivered under an authorised degree-awarding arrangement, participation in a UKAES educational activity does not lead to a UK university degree or a regulated professional qualification.

Any certificate of participation or completion issued by UKAES will describe the educational activity completed and will not be represented as a university degree.
