Education Overview

Advancing Academic Learning and Research Skills.

Education is an important part of the UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences' institutional mission.

UKAES aims to support advanced learning in epidemiology, public health, biostatistics and related scientific disciplines.

Our educational approach emphasises scientific reasoning, methodological knowledge and the practical understanding of research.
