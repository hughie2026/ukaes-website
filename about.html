Who We Are

An Independent Academy for Epidemiological Sciences.

The UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences (UKAES) is an independent academic and research institution dedicated to the advancement of epidemiological sciences and related scientific disciplines.

The Academy focuses on scientific research and development, academic education, professional training and academic development.

Through research, educational activities, scholarly communication and international collaboration, UKAES seeks to advance scientific knowledge and contribute to the improvement of population health.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our academic direction is guided by scientific excellence, independent inquiry and a commitment to the advancement of knowledge.

01 / OUR MISSION

Advancing Scientific Knowledge

To advance epidemiological sciences through scientific research, academic education and interdisciplinary collaboration.

The Academy seeks to promote rigorous scientific methods, strengthen research capabilities and contribute to the development of knowledge relevant to human and population health.

02 / OUR VISION

Building an International Academic Community

To develop as an internationally engaged academic institution recognised for contributions to epidemiological research, scientific education and academic development.

UKAES aspires to foster an academic environment that connects researchers, encourages scientific discovery and supports responsible knowledge exchange.

Institutional Governance

A Focused and Efficient Academic Structure.

UKAES adopts a streamlined approach to institutional development, with academic leadership supported by three principal functional areas.

DIVISION 01

Research

Scientific research, research development, research collaboration, methodology and scientific outputs.

DIVISION 02

Education

Academic education, research-oriented learning, professional training, continuing education and educational development.

DIVISION 03

Academic Affairs

Academic appointments, fellowships, scholarly communication, academic collaboration and institutional academic activities.

This framework describes the Academy's functional organisation. Formal corporate governance and statutory responsibilities remain subject to the institution's governing documents and applicable law.

Our Principles

The Values That Guide Us

The Academy's academic development is guided by a commitment to scientific responsibility and rigorous scholarship.

Scientific Integrity

Upholding honesty, transparency, reproducibility and responsible scientific conduct.

Academic Excellence

Academic Independence

Supporting independent scientific judgement and evidence-based inquiry.

Research Innovation

Encouraging new scientific approaches and interdisciplinary research methods.

Educational Development

Supporting advanced learning, research skills and professional academic growth.

International Collaboration

Promoting scientific exchange and constructive engagement across institutions and disciplines.
