About UKAES

The UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences (UKAES) is an independent academic institution established in 2026.

As a young and pioneering research and education institution, UKAES is committed to advancing scientific knowledge, encouraging interdisciplinary research and supporting academic development.

We embrace emerging technologies, computational approaches and new perspectives in scientific exploration.
