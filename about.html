Who We Are

About UKAES

The UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences (UKAES) is an independent academic institution established in 2026.

As a young and pioneering research and education institution, UKAES is committed to advancing epidemiological sciences, promoting scientific investigation and supporting academic development.

We seek to encourage new perspectives in the study of disease patterns, population health and the determinants of human health.
