Academic Recognition

Two Distinct Selection Systems.

UKAES operates two independent academic selection frameworks. Membership and Fellowship recognise academic and professional standing, while Research Academic Appointments assess qualifications, research capability and institutional academic responsibilities.

PART I

Membership and Fellowship

Selection based on academic qualifications, professional competence, scholarly contributions, leadership and professional influence.

M1

Associate Member

Relevant postgraduate student, bachelor's graduate or early-career practitioner with demonstrable interest in relevant sciences.

M2

Member

Relevant degree or assessed equivalent competence, with evidence of professional practice, research participation or scholarly engagement.

M3

Senior Member

Normally at least five years of relevant experience, sustained professional contributions, research or programme participation, and service.

M4

Fellow

Normally at least eight years of relevant experience, substantial scholarly or professional achievement, original contributions, influence and leadership.

M5

Distinguished Fellow

Normally at least fifteen years of exceptional contribution, major sustained impact, recognised leadership and significant disciplinary or international influence.

HONORARY

Honorary Fellow

A separate discretionary academic honour recognising exceptional contributions. It is not a routine progression grade.

Assessment Framework

Different assessment weightings apply to the membership and fellowship categories.

Assessment Criterion Member / Senior Member Fellow / Distinguished Fellow
Qualifications and Professional Competence 25% 10%
Research Output and Scholarly Quality 20% 35%
Professional Practice and Societal Contribution 30% 15%
Academic Leadership and Service 15% 25%
International Collaboration and Influence 10% 15%

Associate Member Eligibility Screening

Fellow 80 / 100
Distinguished Fellow 90 / 100 + External Academic Support

Supporting Evidence

Applicants are expected to provide relevant documentation, including:

  • Current curriculum vitae and evidence of education or professional competence.
  • Selected scholarly outputs, research contributions, projects or professional achievements.
  • Personal statement explaining suitability and contribution to the Academy.
  • Fellow and Distinguished Fellow candidates: supporting nominations or references and independent scholarly review.

Selection Principle

Experience thresholds are UKAES guidance rather than statutory UK requirements. Scores guide rather than replace reasoned peer judgement. Academic quality, originality, integrity and attributable contribution are assessed rather than publication counts alone.

PART II

Research Academic Appointments

Academic appointment selection evaluates doctoral qualifications, research experience, original scholarship, research independence and academic leadership.

R1

Research Fellow

Relevant doctoral degree (PhD or equivalent). No minimum postdoctoral experience requirement.

R2

Senior Research Fellow

Doctoral degree plus at least one year of postdoctoral or equivalent relevant research experience.

R3

Research Associate Professor

Doctoral degree plus at least four years of relevant research experience.

R4

Research Professor

Doctoral degree plus at least six years of relevant research experience.

Academic Expectations

Academic Grade Expected Academic Standard
Research Fellow Sound research methodology, scholarly integrity, substantive contribution to research projects and verifiable academic or scientific outputs.
Senior Research Fellow Sustained research activity, independent completion of substantial tasks, quality outputs and collaborative or project coordination capability.
Research Associate Professor Sustained original scholarship, research independence, significant outputs and emerging leadership in research supervision or academic collaboration.
Research Professor Substantial original research contributions, significant scholarly influence, established research leadership and a credible plan to advance UKAES research programmes and partnerships.

Research Appointment Assessment

All research academic appointments are assessed against the following weighted criteria.

30% Original Research Contribution and Quality
20% Research Independence and Methodological Capability
20% Academic Leadership and Project Management
15% Scholarly Influence and International Collaboration
10% Proposed Contribution to UKAES Research
5% Academic Service and Professional Contribution

Indicative Appointment Thresholds

Research Grade Minimum Score Additional Expectation
Research Fellow 65 / 100 Demonstrated research competence
Senior Research Fellow 75 / 100 Independent research capability
Research Associate Professor 85 / 100 Academic leadership capability
Research Professor 90 / 100 Major scholarly contributions and research leadership

Exceptional Academic Appointment

Exceptional researchers may be considered for appointment without satisfying the normal minimum years of research experience, provided they demonstrably meet the substantive academic standards of the relevant grade. Academic quality, research integrity, independence and leadership standards cannot be waived.

The normal doctoral degree requirement remains in place. Exceptional appointments require documented justification, independent academic reviews and appropriate institutional approval.

Institutional Status

These criteria constitute a proposed internal UKAES selection framework. They are not statutory UK academic ranks or government-recognised professional qualifications. Formal adoption is subject to institutional governance approval, legal review and appropriate oversight arrangements.
