Academic Recognition

Recognising Achievement. Supporting Research.

The UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences is developing an institutional framework for research academic appointments and fellowships.

The framework is intended to recognise academic achievement while supporting meaningful research activity, scholarly collaboration and institutional academic development.

Research appointments and fellowship honours are distinct forms of academic recognition, with separate eligibility criteria, responsibilities and assessment procedures.

Institutional Framework

Two Distinct Academic Systems

Research Academic Appointments establish a defined academic relationship with UKAES.

Fellowships and Academic Honours recognise scholarly achievement and contribution.

Neither system independently confers a university degree or an externally regulated professional qualification.

Research Academic Appointments

Research Academic Titles

Four proposed academic levels reflect increasing research achievement, independence and scholarly leadership.

LEVEL 04 / SENIOR ACADEMIC LEADERSHIP

Research Professor

For an established researcher with outstanding scholarly achievements, sustained academic influence and significant research leadership.

Indicative assessment

Major original research contributions, an influential scholarly record, independent research leadership, mentoring and strategic contribution.

LEVEL 03 / ADVANCED RESEARCH LEADERSHIP

Research Associate Professor

For an accomplished researcher with sustained research output, independent academic capability and demonstrated scholarly leadership.

Indicative assessment

Strong original research, independent projects, mentoring, collaboration and a sustained contribution to the discipline.

LEVEL 02 / ESTABLISHED RESEARCH

Senior Research Fellow

For an experienced researcher with a substantial research record, independent capability and contributions to project development.

Indicative assessment

Consistent scientific contributions, methodological expertise, research independence and collaborative or mentoring activity.

LEVEL 01 / RESEARCH APPOINTMENT

Research Fellow

For a researcher with demonstrated academic potential and the capability to undertake or contribute meaningfully to scientific research.

Indicative assessment

Research experience, relevant scientific contributions, methodological competence and a commitment to research integrity.

Fellowships & Academic Honours

Academy Fellowships

Fellowships recognise scholarly achievement and distinguished contributions rather than establishing employment.

DISTINGUISHED RECOGNITION

Distinguished Fellow

Recognition of exceptional academic achievement, major scientific contributions and substantial international scholarly influence.

SENIOR SCHOLARLY RECOGNITION

Senior Fellow

Recognition of sustained research achievement, professional distinction and significant contributions to the discipline.

SCHOLARLY RECOGNITION

Fellow

Recognition of established academic achievement and meaningful contributions to research, education or professional practice.

HONORARY RECOGNITION

Honorary Fellow

Recognition of exceptional service or distinguished contributions to science, public health, education or society.

Appointment Relationships

Types of Research Appointment

The appointment category must be clearly stated in the appointment letter and public academic directory.

TYPE 01

Substantive Appointment

A formal appointment with documented research responsibilities and a genuine contractual or other defined institutional relationship.

Employment status and remuneration depend on the actual agreement and applicable law.

TYPE 02

Honorary Appointment

A normally unpaid academic association with defined scholarly contributions. The title must explicitly include "Honorary" and must not imply salaried employment.

TYPE 03

Visiting Appointment

A time-limited research or academic association for a visiting scholar with an agreed purpose, duration and institutional responsibilities.

The title must explicitly include "Visiting".

Examples of appropriate title usage: Research Professor at UKAES; Honorary Research Professor at UKAES; Visiting Research Associate Professor at UKAES; Research Fellow at UKAES. The designation must match the individual's actual approved appointment.

Proposed Procedure

Application & Review Process

A transparent review pathway protects the credibility of academic appointments and honours.

01

Expression of Interest

The candidate or nominator submits an enquiry with a proposed title, academic CV and supporting evidence.

02

Eligibility Screening

UKAES reviews the intended appointment relationship, academic evidence, eligibility and potential conflicts of interest.

03

Academic Assessment

Qualified reviewers evaluate research quality, academic contribution, integrity and the criteria for the proposed level.

04

Formal Decision

An authorised decision is recorded. Approved candidates receive written terms and, where appropriate, an entry in the public directory.

Current status: This page describes a proposed institutional framework. It does not announce that applications are open, that regulations have been formally adopted or that any person has received an appointment.
Academic Governance

Clear Terms. Accountable Decisions.

A credible appointment system requires written rules covering eligibility, assessment, conflicts of interest, appointment terms, renewal, withdrawal and appropriate public representation of academic titles.

Honorary and visiting titles should be used in full. Appointees should follow applicable research integrity, confidentiality and conduct requirements.
