Academic Appointments

Membership & Fellowship

The UK Academy of Epidemiology Sciences provides Membership and Fellowship opportunities for individuals with relevant academic backgrounds, professional experience and contributions to scientific development.

CATEGORY 01

Member

Membership recognises individuals with relevant academic qualifications, professional experience or demonstrated interest in epidemiological sciences and related disciplines.

Selection Criteria

  • Relevant academic qualifications or professional background.
  • Interest or experience in research, education or related scientific fields.
  • Commitment to academic development and professional standards.
  • A record of professional integrity and responsible conduct.
CATEGORY 02

Fellow

Fellowship recognises individuals with substantial academic achievements, professional distinction or significant contributions to research, education and scientific advancement.

Selection Criteria

  • Advanced academic qualifications or equivalent professional standing.
  • An established record of scholarly or professional achievements.
  • Significant contributions to research, education or related disciplines.
  • Evidence of academic leadership, professional recognition or wider disciplinary impact.

All appointments are subject to individual academic and professional assessment in accordance with the relevant selection criteria.
