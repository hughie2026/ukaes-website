Membership & Fellowship

UKAES aims to build an international academic community through membership and fellowship, bringing together individuals with an interest in research, education and scientific advancement.

Academic Recognition

Two Forms of Academic Affiliation

Academic Membership

Member

Membership for individuals interested in epidemiological sciences, research, education and related academic fields.

Members form part of the UKAES academic community and may participate in relevant academic activities and knowledge exchange.

Academic Fellowship

Fellow

An academic distinction recognising individuals with significant scholarly, research, educational or professional contributions.

Fellowship reflects academic achievement, professional standing and contribution to the advancement of relevant disciplines.

Appointment Framework

A Commitment to Academic Standards.

Membership and Fellowship appointments may be considered through application or nomination, subject to the Academy's relevant eligibility requirements and academic review.

Each category represents a distinct form of academic affiliation or recognition.
